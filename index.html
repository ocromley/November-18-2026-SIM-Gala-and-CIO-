Keynote Speaker

Mike Zafirovski

CEO / Founder / Board Member / Philanthropist

Mike Zafirovski is the Founder, Chairman, and President of The Zaf Group (TZG), a family office engaged in operating, investment, and advisory activities.

TZG's portfolio includes Tusker, a fast-growing national IT solutions platform based in Buffalo Grove, providing a broad range of advisory, professional, and technology solutions.

During his corporate career, Mike served as President and/or CEO at Nortel, Motorola, and five GE businesses. He served on the boards of Boeing, Stericycle, Apria Healthcare, Nortel, Motorola, and Blackstone portfolio companies.

Mike and his wife Robin established The Zafirovski Family Foundation in 2005, focusing on education, healthcare, faith-based ministries, and economic development.

A native of Macedonia, Mike is the co-founder and director of Macedonia 2025. In partnership with Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management, the Zafirovski Executive Development Program has helped prepare more than 100 future CEOs and senior executives.

2026 CIO of the Year Awards

The 2026 CIO of the Year Awards are among the most prestigious honors in the Chicago technology community. They recognize Chief Information Officers who have demonstrated exceptional leadership, innovation, and impact in their organizations and the broader technology ecosystem.

Selection Criteria:

  • Demonstrated leadership in driving technology strategy and digital transformation
  • Proven ability to deliver business value through innovative technology solutions
  • Commitment to developing talent and building high-performing teams
  • Contributions to the technology community through mentorship and thought leadership
  • Excellence in aligning IT initiatives with organizational goals and objectives

About SIM Chicago

Society for Information Management

SIM is the premier network for IT leaders, comprised of over 5,000 members across 40 chapters in the United States. For over five decades, SIM has been the leading organization for senior IT executives, providing a unique forum for networking, professional development, and thought leadership.

Our Mission:

  • To nurture a community of senior IT executives who support each other in their professional growth
  • To foster meaningful connections between IT leaders across industries
  • To advance the IT profession through knowledge sharing and mentorship
  • To promote diversity and inclusion in technology leadership

SIM Chicago Chapter:

The Chicago chapter is one of SIM's most active communities, hosting regular events that bring together CIOs, CTOs, and senior technology leaders from the region's top organizations. Our CIO of the Year Awards program celebrates the exceptional leaders who are driving innovation and excellence in technology across Chicago.
